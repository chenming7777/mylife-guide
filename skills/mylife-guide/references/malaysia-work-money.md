# Malaysia: work, money, assistance and education

Status: initial referral-level adaptation, checked **2026-11-12**. Covers upstream chapters **5, 7, 12, 15, 19, 23 and 31**, not every item within them. Original chapter numbers identify the topic being adapted; Malaysian guidance below has its own stable IDs. Do not cite a Chinese item as evidence for a Malaysian entitlement.

All linked sources were accessed **2026-11-12**. Source publication/update dates are marked where supplied; “undated” means no reliable content date was established, not that the rule is permanent. Reopen the relevant official page before answering a live financial, benefits or legal question. Agency guidance supports a referral; it does not establish that a particular person qualifies.

First establish state/territory, citizenship or residence status, employee versus contractor status, relevant dates, and the specific scheme or contract. Ask for categories and redacted figures, not MyKad numbers, passwords or complete bank statements.

## MY-W01 — Employment rights and unpaid wages (chapters 7, 19)

**Action and scope:** Keep the employment contract, payslips, working-hour records, correspondence and termination letter. For unpaid wages or other employment payments, approach the labour department responsible for the workplace. JTKSM's labour-case process covers financial claims and asks claimants to bring supporting documents to the nearest relevant Labour Office (PTK); filing is free. Confirm the applicable entitlement before calculating it.

The Employment Act 1955 framework applies in **Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan**. Sabah and Sarawak have their own Labour Ordinances. Their 2025 amendments do not justify applying every Employment Act provision or wage-based exception unchanged. Employment status, wage level and work category can affect particular protections. Check the current minimum-wage order and its exclusions through the relevant department rather than reusing an old salary figure.

For **Sabah**, use [JTK Sabah's complaint and advice routes](https://www.jtksabah.gov.my/utama/perkhidmatan-kami/aduan-buruh-dan-khidmat-nasihat/) (the linked Sabah Labour Complaints Portal, email or a nearby Labour Office). Its [branch directory](https://www.jtksabah.gov.my/utama/info-korporat/pejabat-cawangan/) lists **PTK Kota Kinabalu: 088-295900, jtkbkinabalu@mohr.gov.my**; confirm the office location before visiting because the directory displays differing address text. For **Sarawak**, [JTK Sarawak's labour-complaint guide](https://www.jtkswk.gov.my/v2/?page_id=958) provides the complaint form and routes to the office nearest the workplace, including email, post, telephone and an in-person visit. These are complaint/advice channels; unfair-dismissal representation remains a separate route under MY-W02. All three pages are undated; checked 2026-11-12.

**Avoid importing:** China's overtime formula, “double salary” for an unsigned contract, nationwide labour-arbitration procedure, or a single deadline for all claims.

**Sources:** [JTKSM labour-case FAQ](https://jtksm.gov.my/en/frequently-asked-questions/labour-case-frequently-asked-questions) (undated); [JTKSM 2022 amendment overview](https://jtksm.gov.my/sites/default/files/2023-03/Akta%20Kerja%20%28Pindaan%29%202022.pdf) (effective 2023-01-01); [Sabah amendment FAQ](https://jtksabah.gov.my/images/warta_2025/SOALAN_LAZIM.pdf) (2025 amendments); [Sarawak labour FAQ](https://www.jtkswk.gov.my/v2/?page_id=997) (undated, amendments effective 2025-05-01); [minimum-wage FAQ](https://www.jtkswk.gov.my/v2/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/FAQ-PGM-2024.pdf) (2024 order, phased implementation in 2025).

## MY-W02 — Dismissal: separate the remedies (chapters 7, 19)

**Action and scope:** Record the dismissal date and distinguish unpaid contractual/statutory payments, unfair-dismissal representation, and EIS benefits. These are separate routes. JPPM states that a worker alleging dismissal without just cause or excuse may submit a section 20 representation **within 60 days of dismissal**, including during the notice period, through IRIS or the relevant office. Confirm the appropriate remedy and deadline promptly with JPPM; negotiating with the employer does not establish that a filing deadline has stopped.

**Avoid importing:** Chinese “N / N+1 / 2N” severance formulas, automatic compensation, or promises that a labour case finishes within a fixed number of months.

**Source:** [JPPM dismissal representations](https://jpp.mohr.gov.my/en/ems-dismissal-seksyen-20/) (undated).

## MY-W03 — PERKESO/SOCSO and EIS/SIP (chapters 7, 19, 31)

**Action and scope:** Check contribution records and contact PERKESO for the applicable protection and required evidence. Employment injury, invalidity, self-employment protection and EIS are different schemes. For eligible insured persons, EIS requires application **within 60 days of loss of employment**, sufficient qualifying contributions, and a loss falling within the Act's definition. Use PERKESO's linked LINDUNG KERJAYA portal and re-employment services. Do not assume resignation, contract expiry, self-employment or every foreign-worker category qualifies.

Citizenship/residence, age, contract type and contribution history need scheme-specific confirmation. Some official summaries differ on domestic-worker and residence categories; do not resolve these differences by guessing. For a workplace injury, obtain care, preserve incident/medical records and seek PERKESO's current claim instructions promptly.

**Avoid importing:** China's social-insurance transfer process, work-injury assessment deadlines or payout multipliers.

**Sources:** [PERKESO EIS requirements and application](https://www.perkeso.gov.my/en/uncategorised/925-employment-insurance-system.html); [PERKESO scheme-specific FAQ](https://www.perkeso.gov.my/en/contact-us/pejabat-perkeso-new/frequently-asked-question) (both undated).

## MY-W04 — EPF/KWSP retirement savings (chapters 5, 19, 31)

**Action and scope:** Compare payslip deductions with the member's EPF record and ask KWSP to investigate missing contributions. Verify contribution category and the current rules for any proposed withdrawal. Retirement savings, social insurance and housing finance are distinct.

Do not assume EPF is only for citizens: KWSP states mandatory contributions for covered non-Malaysian employees began with **October 2025 wages**. Its guidance specifies valid documentation, age and domestic-worker exclusions; existing membership categories can matter. Check the applicable category before quoting rates.

**Avoid importing:** China's housing provident fund loan, pension contribution-year threshold or pension “break-even” calculation as a Malaysian rule.

**Source:** [KWSP non-Malaysian employee contributions](https://www.kwsp.gov.my/en/employer/responsibilities/non-malaysian-citizen-employees) (undated; implementation from 2025-10-01).

## MY-W05 — Tax and cross-border income (chapters 12, 19, 31)

**Action and scope:** Establish the year of assessment, tax residence, income sources and whether a business is carried on before choosing a return. Use HASiL's MyTax/e-Daftar and current filing guidance. Tax residence follows section 7 tests; nationality or simply counting one year's 182 days is not a complete determination. Remote work for an overseas employer is not automatically tax-free. Check relevant source, treaty and exemption rules with HASiL or a qualified adviser.

**Avoid importing:** Chinese personal-income-tax brackets, severance exemptions, annual filing dates or rules for receiving foreign payments. Budget announcements and prior-year relief tables are not substitutes for the applicable assessment-year rules.

**Sources:** [HASiL individual-tax introduction](https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/individu/pengenalan-cukai-pendapatan-individu/); [section 7 residence tests](https://www.hasil.gov.my/individu/taraf-mastautin/); [TIN and MyTax FAQ](https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/individu/soalan-lazim-individu/) (all undated).

## MY-W06 — Financial hardship and public assistance (chapter 7)

**Action and scope:** Identify immediate food, accommodation and household needs; contact the relevant district JKM office or use the official eBantuan route. Prepare identity, household and income evidence requested for the chosen programme. Assistance is assessed; unemployment alone does not establish eligibility.

For STR/SARA, use the official programme-year FAQ and check citizenship, household category, income, approval phase and any eKasih requirement. Distinguish monthly SARA cashless purchases from cash payments and one-off programmes. On the check date, the STR portal already displayed **2027 FAQs**: do not treat a saved 2026 table as current for every application.

**Avoid importing:** Chinese minimum-living allowance, hukou requirements, universal unemployment grants or guaranteed benefit amounts.

**Sources:** [MyGovernment JKM application guide](https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/topics/memohon-bantuan-jkm); [HASiL STR programme-year FAQs](https://bantuantunai.hasil.gov.my/Info/FAQ) (both undated); [SARA 2026 FAQ](https://www.sara.gov.my/documents/sara2026_faqs_final_20260108.pdf) (2026-01-08; verify the relevant programme year).

## MY-W07 — Debt repayment difficulties (chapters 5, 7)

**Action and scope:** List balances, effective costs, due dates and affordable repayments. Contact the lender early using its official channel. BNM directs borrowers to bank repayment assistance and AKPK debt-management routes; eligibility and revised terms require assessment. Retain written offers and check total repayment cost before accepting.

**Avoid importing:** Chinese credit-reporting periods, guaranteed interest cancellation, or paid intermediaries' promises to erase debt. A referral is not approval and does not itself suspend payments or proceedings.

**Source:** [BNM repayment-assistance guidance](https://www.bnm.gov.my/ra) (undated; current page references 2026 assistance).

## MY-W08 — Deposits and PIDM (chapter 5)

**Action and scope:** Check that both the bank and product qualify. PIDM protects eligible deposits up to **RM250,000 per depositor per member bank**, including principal and interest/return. Accounts at different branches of the same bank are aggregated; eligible Islamic and conventional deposits have separate limits. Stocks, unit trusts and gold investment accounts are not insured deposits. Nationality does not itself prevent deposit-insurance coverage.

**Avoid importing:** China's deposit-insurance limit or treating every bank-sold product as a deposit. PIDM deposit protection does not promise reimbursement for investment losses or scams.

**Source:** [PIDM deposit-insurance FAQ](https://www.pidm.gov.my/general/faqs/deposit-insurance-system) (undated).

## MY-W09 — Money transferred to a scammer (chapters 5, 7, 12)

**Action and scope:** Immediately contact the bank through its verified scam hotline and **NSRC 997**, preserve transaction details/messages, and make a police report. PDRM's dated 2026 notice says 997 operates **24 hours daily, including public holidays**. Contact authorities even if reporting was delayed; do not promise recovery. Never send another payment to “unlock” recovered funds.

**Avoid importing:** Chinese anti-fraud numbers, recovery guarantees or a rule that reporting after 24 hours is pointless. BNM's undated FAQ still showed older operating hours when checked; use the newer dated PDRM notice for hours and recheck future changes.

**Sources:** [PDRM NSRC notice](https://www.rmp.gov.my/news-detail/2026/04/27/besmartstayalert-letsfightscammertogether-postings-pilihan-nsrc-997-perangi-jenayah-dan-penipuan) (2026-04-27); [BNM scam-response FAQ](https://www.bnm.gov.my/faqs/scams) (undated).

## MY-W10 — Starting a business (chapters 12, 31)

**Action and scope:** Choose the legal form, location and activity before registration. SSM's EzBiz sole-proprietor/partnership route requires Malaysian citizenship or permanent residence and age 18 or above. Registration under the Registration of Businesses Act covers Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan; do not apply that route unchanged to Sabah/Sarawak business addresses. Company/LLP and foreign-owner cases need their own checks. Separately confirm premises/activity licences, tax obligations and written partner arrangements.

**Avoid importing:** China's individual-business registration, annual report calendar, invoice system, registered-capital assumptions or startup-loan guarantees.

**Sources:** [SSM EzBiz requirements](https://ssm.com.my/Pages/Services/Registration-of-Business-%28ROB%29/EzBiz-Online.aspx); [SSM territorial scope](https://crmsv.ssm4u.com.my/ssmkb/SSM4UExternal?name=business+registration&page=3) (both undated).

## MY-W11 — Housing dispute triage (chapter 15)

**Action and scope:** Preserve the signed agreement, payment records, notices and dated photographs. Identify whether the dispute concerns a tenancy, developer sale agreement or strata management. KPKT's homebuyer tribunal concerns qualifying buyer/developer sale-agreement claims; its strata tribunal is a different route. An ordinary rental-deposit dispute is not automatically a homebuyer claim. The Judiciary identifies landlord–tenant disputes as civil matters; ask the relevant court registry about procedure and eligibility for a small claim where appropriate. Verify state, legal scope, amount and deadline before filing.

**Avoid importing:** Chinese rental eviction rules, housing provident-fund mortgages, property-registration procedures, tribunal limits or guaranteed completion dates. Sabah/Sarawak housing routes require separate jurisdiction checks.

**Sources:** [KPKT housing and strata tribunal functions](https://www.kpkt.gov.my/index.php/pages/view/438); [Judiciary civil-case guidance](https://www.kehakiman.gov.my/ms/prosedur-bagi-kes-sivil?q=en%2Fprocedures-civil-cases) (both undated).

## MY-W12 — Qualifications, training and study finance (chapters 23, 31)

**Action and scope:** Before paying, check the exact programme, institution, campus and accreditation period in MQA's MQR. If absent, check provisional accreditation; absence from MQR alone is not proof of fraud. Compare total fees, living costs, lost earnings and actual entry requirements. UP_TVET provides course prerequisites, duration, fees and allowances; each course/institution sets eligibility. Do not infer a universal age limit from inconsistent FAQ summaries.

PTPTN financing requires its own application and eligibility checks, including Malaysian citizenship and a qualifying registered course. Accreditation alone does not guarantee financing. Confirm the intake and repayment conditions before enrolment.

**Avoid importing:** Gaokao, 学信网, Chinese vocational subsidies, uniform tuition grants or guaranteed employment from a certificate.

**Sources:** [MQA programme search and scope](https://www.mqa.gov.my/mqr/carianakr.cfm); [UP_TVET FAQ/catalogue route](https://mohon.tvet.gov.my/main/faq); [PTPTN education-loan conditions](https://www.ptptn.gov.my/en/pinjaman-pendidikan/) (all undated).
