# Malaysia: health, emergencies and family services

The dates below describe the cited source when it gives one; the check date is not the publication date. ## MY-H01 — Get emergency help through Malaysia's emergency system

**Action:** For an immediate life-threatening emergency in Malaysia, call **999**. Give the location and follow the emergency service's instructions. The Ministry of Health describes 999 as the coordinated route for emergency ambulance response; Malaysian emergency services include medical, police and fire/rescue response. An emergency does not require a MySejahtera appointment before seeking care. At a health facility, staff assess urgency through triage.

**Cost and benefit:** Calling and explaining the situation takes time; the benefit is reaching the appropriate emergency service. No ambulance response time or charge is promised here.

**Replace:** Do not repeat the source book's China emergency numbers or assume a Chinese hospital triage classification applies locally. This entry changes the contact pathway, not the clinical first-aid instructions. Use current Malaysian or international first-aid guidance appropriate to the actual emergency.

**Scope:** Malaysia. Some specialist hospitals do not provide a public emergency department, so do not tell a user to attend an arbitrary hospital solely because it is nearby.

**Sources:** [MOH statement on the 999 emergency service](https://www.moh.gov.my/moh/resources/kenyatan%20akhbar/Kenyataan%20Akhbar%20KPK/KENYATAAN_AKHBAR_KPK_PANGGILAN_KECEMASAN_MERS_999_22_Oktober_2015.pdf) (22 October 2015); [MySejahtera emergency walk-in FAQ](https://helpdesk.mysejahtera.malaysia.gov.my/en/support/solutions/articles/51000327126-can-i-walk-in-to-the-health-facility-for-an-emergency-without-booking-any-appointment-slot-) (modified 10 July 2024); [Hospital Rehabilitasi Cheras service scope](https://jknkl.moh.gov.my/hrc/ukep/) (page date not established). Checked 2026-11-12.

## MY-H02 — Use a local clinic and the appropriate referral pathway

**Action:** For non-emergency assessment, contact a suitable Klinik Kesihatan or other local clinic. MOH's general specialist-care pathway starts with assessment by a medical officer at a government or private clinic/hospital, followed by referral where appropriate. Confirm the receiving clinic's appointment and referral requirements. MySejahtera supports appointments for services offered by participating facilities; confirm availability with the facility when needed.

**Cost and benefit:** Allow for an initial consultation, travel and possible separate specialist appointment. The benefit is an appropriate assessment and referral. Do not promise a fixed waiting time, access to a particular drug or admission.

**Replace:** China's community-hospital/tertiary-hospital price comparisons, family-doctor contracts, cross-province reimbursement and named Chinese insurance arrangements are not Malaysian entitlements. A Chinese recommendation for a screening age or frequency also needs a current Malaysian clinical source before being presented as a local programme.

**Sources:** [MOH treatment and hospital FAQ](https://www.moh.gov.my/en/component/content/article/faq?catid=2) (page publication date not established); [MySejahtera service-appointment explanation](https://helpdesk.myvas.moh.gov.my/en/support/solutions/articles/51000348607-what-is-the-difference-if-i-enable-the-services-for-mysj-walk-in-and-myvas-) (date not established). Checked 2026-11-12.

## MY-H03 — Check charges against the patient's actual status

**Action:** Ask the receiving hospital or clinic about its applicable charges, deposits and exemptions. State whether the patient is a Malaysian citizen, a non-citizen or in another qualifying category, and identify the service, ward class and referral route. Check insurance coverage separately with the insurer or employer.

**Cost and benefit:** A fee inquiry can prevent a misleading estimate. Public-hospital costs are not a single universal flat charge; investigations and procedures may be charged separately from consultations or ward charges. Do not substitute a currency conversion for checking a Malaysian fee schedule.

**Replace:** Do not import Chinese medical-insurance copayments, reimbursement ratios, free-drug programmes or emergency-aid eligibility. This guide does not establish that every service is free, that all residents receive citizen rates, or that a deposit equals the final bill.

**Sources:** [MOH treatment and hospital FAQ](https://www.moh.gov.my/en/component/content/article/faq?catid=2); [Hospital Limbang non-citizen charges](https://jknsarawak.moh.gov.my/hlimbang/caj-bukan-warganegara/). Dates not established; checked 2026-11-12. No fixed fee is copied here because it must match the receiving facility and service.

## MY-H04 — Route mental-health support by urgency

**Action:** Respond supportively and without judgment. An immediate danger belongs in the emergency pathway in MY-H01. For non-urgent new cases, MENTARI's official FAQ says to arrange an appointment before attending and says a health-clinic referral letter is not required to arrange that appointment. Its FAQ advises people previously treated elsewhere to seek continuity with that service; confirm the local centre's intake arrangements.

**Current support contact:** The Malaysia helpline lookup returned **Talian HEAL**, [15555](tel:15555?oai_link_source=model_response_hotline), with emotional/psychosocial support and early crisis intervention, operating daily **8:00 am to midnight**. Official website returned by that lookup: [https://sites.google.com/moh.gov.my/ncemh/talian-sokongan-psikososial](https://sites.google.com/moh.gov.my/ncemh/talian-sokongan-psikososial?oai_link_source=model_response_hotline). Lookup date: **2026-11-12**. Refresh the dedicated helpline lookup before giving a crisis number in a future conversation; these details are not an availability guarantee.

**Replace:** China's national psychological-assistance number and regional youth services are not Malaysia contacts. Do not substitute a routine clinic appointment or a cost-benefit ranking for urgent help.

**Sources:** Dedicated Malaysia helpline lookup above; [MENTARI official FAQ](https://mentari.moh.gov.my/frequently-asked-question/) (date not established, checked 2026-11-12).

## MY-H05 — Check medicines and cosmetics through NPRA

**Action:** Search the exact product in **NPRA QUEST3+**. For medicines, check the product name, registration number and active registration status, rather than trusting a seller's logo or a photograph of a number. Cosmetics have a separate notification category. Read the Malaysian product's consumer information leaflet, **RiMUP**, and consult a pharmacist or prescriber about suitability.

**Cost and benefit:** The registry check takes time and helps establish regulatory status. Registration or notification is not a guarantee that a particular product suits a particular patient, nor proof of every advertising claim. QUEST3+ expressly says search results apply at the time of the search and status can change.

**Replace:** Chinese medicine approvals, cosmetic registration identifiers, prescription availability and drug-price claims do not establish Malaysian status. An ingredient name alone does not establish the strength, formulation, child dose or interactions of the product in hand.

**Sources:** [NPRA registration-check instructions](https://npra.gov.my/index.php/en/consumers/reporting/quality-reporting-on-registered-product.html); [QUEST3+ product search](https://quest3plus.bpfk.gov.my/pmo2/index.php); [NPRA notified-cosmetic reporting](https://www.npra.gov.my/index.php/en/consumers/reporting/reporting-on-notified-cosmetic-products.html). Dates not established; checked 2026-11-12.

## MY-H06 — Check individual medicine precautions, especially with kidney disease

**Action:** For a medicine question, identify the actual Malaysian product and read its current leaflet. A Malaysian ibuprofen leaflet, for example, tells patients to disclose kidney, liver or heart problems and other medicines before use. Use that as a prompt for a pharmacist or clinician review, not as permission to prescribe an alternative or calculate an unsourced dose.

**Replace:** Do not carry over blanket over-the-counter assumptions, fixed treatment durations or Chinese disposal categories. The cited Malaysian Bifen leaflet directs unused suspension back to a pharmacist and says not to put it into normal household drainage or waste; ask the pharmacist how to return the particular medicine.

**Scope:** The cited leaflets concern specific ibuprofen products. They do not provide a complete chronic-kidney-disease regimen, universal painkiller advice, or paediatric dosing for other products.

**Sources:** [NPRA-hosted Nufen RiMUP](https://quest3plus.bpfk.gov.my/front-end/attachment/637/pharma/226610/V_97312_20240828_171731_D4.pdf) (revision date not established from the inspected text); [NPRA-hosted Bifen RiMUP](https://quest3plus.bpfk.gov.my/front-end/attachment/4556/pharma/223009/V_80453_20230523_112131_D4.pdf) (revision 16 May 2023). Checked 2026-11-12; retrieve the current product leaflet at use.

## MY-H07 — Arrange Malaysian antenatal and child-health care

**Action:** Contact a government health clinic for pregnancy registration and follow-up. The MyGovernment maternal-health page recommends registration before 12 weeks. For a baby or child, use the current MOH National Immunisation Programme and keep the child's health record. Ask the clinic about missed vaccines, catch-up care and the child's particular circumstances.

**Cost and benefit:** Expect appointments and travel. Do not promise all care or vaccinations are free for every citizenship category or private provider. Obtain the relevant facility's advice for fees and eligibility.

**Replace:** China's antenatal registration steps, birth-related subsidies, mandatory checks, vaccine schedule and insurance arrangements are not Malaysia rules. Do not copy a Chinese vaccine timetable or infer citizenship from birth in Malaysia.

**Sources:** [MyGovernment maternal health](https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/topics/kesihatan-ibu-hamil) (updated 14 May 2026); [MyGovernment child immunisation](https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/categories/health/babies-and-children/child-immunisation-programme) (updated 28 January 2026). Official indexed text checked 2026-11-12; reconfirm clinical schedules with MOH/clinic.

## MY-H08 — Use JPN's regional birth and death procedures

**Action:** Select the applicable **Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah or Sarawak** procedure on the National Registration Department (**JPN**) site before stating a deadline, form or required document. Distinguish ordinary registration from late registration, a certificate extract, and an event abroad.

**Cost and benefit:** Preparing the correct documents reduces repeat visits. This entry does not establish citizenship, inheritance entitlement, funeral subsidies, estate access or a nationwide registration deadline.

**Replace:** Chinese household registration, deregistration, social-insurance death payments and succession procedures must not be repackaged as Malaysian rules. A death certificate is not by itself authority to distribute an estate.

**Sources:** [JPN birth-service directory](https://www.jpn.gov.my/en/services/birth/); [JPN death-service directory](https://www.jpn.gov.my/en/services/death). Page dates not established; checked 2026-11-12.

## MY-H09 — Check Malaysian disability registration and support separately

**Action:** Use **JKM MyDaftarOKU** or the relevant district welfare office for OKU registration. The official application is for Malaysian citizens and describes medical verification/document requirements; its process also distinguishes obvious and non-obvious disabilities. Follow the application instructions appropriate to the case.

**Cost and benefit:** Documents and assessment take time. An OKU card can support access to relevant facilities, but it does not establish automatic eligibility for every cash payment, device, education or employment benefit. Check each scheme separately.

**Replace:** China's disability certificates, disability grades, rehabilitation allowances and work-injury compensation are different schemes. Do not promise that a non-citizen qualifies for JKM registration or infer inability to work from disability status.

**Source:** [JKM MyDaftarOKU application and instructions](https://oku.jkm.gov.my/myDaftarOku). Date not established; checked 2026-11-12.

## MY-H10 — Ask JKM about support for children, older people and care needs

**Action:** Match the circumstances to the relevant scheme, then use its current JKM application channel or district/division welfare office. **BKK** concerns qualifying children and families; **BWE** concerns qualifying older people; **BPT** concerns eligible care arrangements and chronic illness. These are assessed schemes, not automatic payments based solely on age, parenthood or diagnosis.

**Scope:** The cited scheme pages specify Malaysian citizenship and residence, with scheme-specific income and other conditions. BWE includes an age threshold and institutional-care exclusions. BKK has exclusions involving some other assistance and certain working older children. BPT distinguishes carers from chronic patients applying in their own name. Read the full current criteria before saying a person qualifies.

**Cost and benefit:** Gathering evidence and assessment take time; a successful application may help with living or care costs. No amount is promised here. The BPT page mixes the current listed rate with a **Budget 2027** increase announcement: do not treat a future budget announcement as an already effective payment rule.

**Replace:** Do not convert Chinese child allowances, long-term-care insurance, elderly subsidies or disability benefits into RM or present them as equivalent Malaysian schemes.

**Sources:** MOF Portal Manfaat: [BKK](https://manfaat.mof.gov.my/bantuan/JKM-005), [BWE](https://manfaat.mof.gov.my/bantuan/JKM-001), [BPT](https://manfaat.mof.gov.my/bantuan/JKM-003). Each displayed updated date **11 October 2026**; official indexed text checked 2026-11-12. Direct page retrieval was restricted, so amounts and final eligibility require confirmation with JKM.
